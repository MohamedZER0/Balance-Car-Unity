## Advertising and Opt-Out

### Personalized Ads
Our App displays personalized advertisements based on your interests. These ads are provided by Google AdMob.

### How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

**Android:**
1. Go to Settings > Google > Ads
2. Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

**Alternative:** You can reset your advertising ID to limit tracking:
1. Go to Settings > Google > Ads
2. Select "Reset advertising ID"

**Note:** Opting out does not mean you will see fewer ads, but the ads you see may be less relevant to you.
